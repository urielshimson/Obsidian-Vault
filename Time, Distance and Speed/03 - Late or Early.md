A) 35 mins
B) 30 mins
C) 25 mins

**🎯 [[Time, Distance and Speed/pdfs/02 - SET-1 (TDS problems) Part 2.pdf#page=4|Problem 16:]]**

**If I walk at 3 kmph, I miss the train by 2 minutes. If, however, I walk at 4 kmph, I reach the station 2 minutes before the arrival of the train. How far do I walk to reach the station?**

==A) 4/5​ km==
B) 5/4 km
C) 6/5​ km
D) 3/4 km

- In both the Initial case (Late) and the Final case (Early), the walking Distance will be the same.

**⏱️ Time difference**
- Miss keyword → Use + sign.
- Early/Before Keyword → Use - sign.
