Israel** is a _country_ in the Middle East.  
It’s located on the eastern side of the Mediterranean Sea.

### 🕍 **Why is Israel important?**

- It is the **historic homeland of the Jewish people**.
    
- Many events in the **Bible** happened in this region.
    
- It’s home to Jerusalem, a city sacred to **Jews, Christians, and Muslims**.
    

### 🌎 **When was modern Israel formed?**

The modern State of Israel was established in **1948**, after World War II.

### 👥 **Who lives there?**

Mostly:

- **Jews**
    
- **Arab Muslims**
    
- **Arab Christians**
    
- Druze and other minorities
    

### 💡 **What kind of country is it?**

- A **democratic country**
    
- Highly developed in technology, agriculture, medicine, and defense
