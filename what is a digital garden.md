A **digital garden** is like your own personal corner of the internet where you grow your ideas, notes, and learnings — slowly, over time — just like nurturing small plants in a garden.

### 🌿 What makes it special?

- **Not a blog:** A blog has finished, polished posts.  
    A digital garden has **unfinished notes**, ideas you’re still thinking about.
    
- **You update anytime:** Notes can change, grow, improve — just like adding water to plants.
    
- **Interlinked:** Pages connect to each other with links, like branches.  
    (Obsidian is perfect for this.)
    
- **More personal:** It’s not about followers. It’s about your learning journey.
    

### 🌱 Why people love digital gardens?

- They are free-flowing, no pressure to be perfect.
    
- They let you show your knowledge and thoughts in an organized, evolving way.
    
- Your vault becomes an online public notebook.
    

In Obsidian, tools like **Flowershow** or **Quartz** turn your notes into a beautiful digital garden website.
